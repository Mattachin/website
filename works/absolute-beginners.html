Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ teaching

Absolute Beginners – Introduction to Experimental Radio

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, 2023–2025

Cross-chair introductory module of the sound area, Faculty of Art and Design

With Nathalie Singer, Jason Langheim and Frederike Moormann

WiSe 2023/24 – WiSe 2025/26

An introductory module to radio and sound practice shared by the chairs of the sound area. Through practical exercises in field recording, acoustics, radio art, editing in Reaper, sound collage and studio production, students made their first audio works, ending in a live broadcast on bauhaus.fm.

In later editions the course was built around a short artistic feature of five to seven minutes, moving from research, exposé and interviews to recording on location, writing for listening and editing in the studio.
