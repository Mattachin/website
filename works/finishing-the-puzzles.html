Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ teaching

Finishing the Puzzles – vier Eingriffe, bevor es kippt!

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, SoSe 2024

Experimental Radio, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

With Nathalie Singer, Jason Langheim and Frederike Moormann; guests Schorsch Kamerun and Tilmann Böhnke

SoSe 2024

An artistic radio project creating interventions in public space around protest, democracy and shared listening. Over the semester students developed interviews, editing and broadcast technique towards four interventions. One of them, Tafelfunk, transmitted a shared meal at the Weimar food bank into the sound systems of supermarkets.
