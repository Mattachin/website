Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ teaching

Listening to the World – Attuning to Archives

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, WiSe 2022/23

Experimental Radio, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

With Nathalie Singer and Frederike Moormann

WiSe 2022/23

A seminar within the Listening to the World project, exploring how listening connects places, histories and perspectives. Students worked with radio archives, listened to the radio cultures of different regions, met artists and researchers in conversation, and made their own audio works as part of the research towards 100 years of radio art.
