Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ teaching

FLURFUNK – Radio als Medium des Widerstands

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, SoSe 2026

Experimental Radio, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

With Nathalie Singer, Frederike Moormann and Jason Langheim

Including the FLURFUNK LAB

SoSe 2026

A course on radio as a medium of resistance, through activist radio works, analog and digital transmission, visits to broadcasting infrastructure, communication walks and collective performance. In the FLURFUNK LAB, taught with Jason Langheim, students explored frequency bands and transmission networks through collective experiments and live broadcasts. The work was developed and presented at the Summaery 2026.
