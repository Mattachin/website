Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ artist talk

Mobile devices and live radio improvisations

TACTUS Radio Festival, Usmaradio, San Marino, 2023

Artist talk, in English

Antico Monastero Santa Chiara, Republic of San Marino, 1–3 December 2023

A talk given at the TACTUS Radio Festival, the international radio art festival of Usmaradio, the radio research centre of the Università degli Studi della Repubblica di San Marino. Over three days the festival brought narrations, round tables, performances, listening sessions and live broadcasts into the Antico Monastero Santa Chiara.

At the same festival I performed in the network radio performance CONTINUUM.
