Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ teaching

Wetlands

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, NKUA Athens, Aalto University, WiSe 2025/26

Erasmus Blended Intensive Programme, faculty member for Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

With the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and Aalto University

Fieldwork: Psachna, Greece, 12–16 January 2026

An interdisciplinary course at the meeting point of art, science and ecology, focused on aquatic ecosystems. During a week of fieldwork around the Kolovrechtis Wetland and the Chalcis Strait, participants developed artistic projects and embodied sensory research, using methods from storytelling and creative writing to interactive installations and scientific approaches.

The course was coordinated by the Acoustic Ecologies and Media Environments departments at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar and hosted by DCARTS of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, with the Department of Art and Media at Aalto University.
