CONTINUUM

TACTUS Radio Festival, Usmaradio, San Marino, 2023

Collaborative network radio performance, conceived by Roberto Paci Dalò

Performed from Weimar as part of the Bauhaus Radio Ensemble

Republic of San Marino and worldwide, 2 December 2023

CONTINUUM was a one-hour collaborative radio performance conceived by Roberto Paci Dalò for the TACTUS Radio Festival and broadcast on 2 December 2023 from Usmaradio together with a network of radio stations. Eighteen performers played together, some on site in the Republic of San Marino and others remotely from Santa Cruz, Vienna and Weimar, most of them without having met or spoken before.

The performance was a radical improvisation without conduction or prior rules, held together only by the attention of each performer to the others. It continued the practice of telematic radio performance that developed in the early 1990s around ORF Kunstradio, Ars Electronica and L’Arte dell’Ascolto, bringing independent stations and public broadcasters together again.

At the same festival I gave the talk Mobile devices and live radio improvisations.
