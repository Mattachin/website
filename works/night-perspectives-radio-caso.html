Night Perspectives: Soundscapes of Western Thrace through the Politics of Listening

RADIO CASo, Centro de Arte Sonoro, Buenos Aires, 2025

Durational night broadcasts of field recordings from Western Thrace

Five nights, 25–31 December 2025, from midnight Buenos Aires time

RADIO CASo, Centro de Arte Sonoro, Buenos Aires

Soundscapes of Western Thrace through the Politics of Listening is an ongoing artistic PhD research project focused on the sonic environment of the Greek borderland in Western Thrace, bordering Turkey and Bulgaria. The research utilizes various methods of listening and field recording to investigate the political and social structures that shape the sonic environment of these porous territories.

In this curated selection of durational listening for RADIO CASo, we tune into the night perspectives of the border. We move from the social signals of twilight into the deep silence of the night.

  • 25 December 2025 Komotini – sunset, end of the fasting day, 3 March 2025 (6 h 20 min)
  • 26 December 2025 Dadia Forest, from evening to night, spring 2025 (1 h)
  • 27 December 2025 Dadia Forest, Easter, village echoes (1 h)
  • 30 December 2025 Tychero, February 2025, deep night (7 h)
  • 31 December 2025 Dadia Forest, next to the E85, across the border (10 h 40 min)
  • Also in the archive Mikro Dereio, September 2024 (7 h 50 min)
