Experimental Radio, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

SoSe 2023

A course in acoustic ecology and soundscape design. Students studied listening and soundscape analysis, mapped and documented sonic environments, and considered how sound relates to health, society, culture and place, before developing their own creative responses.
