B.A.S.E Tsonami, Valparaíso, 2023

Micro-residency at B.A.S.E Tsonami, Valparaíso, Chile

1–10 April 2023

From the 1st until the 10th of April 2023 I was in a micro-residency at B.A.S.E, the space of Tsonami Arte Sonoro in Valparaíso. It was a short investigation of the sound environment of the city, through improvisation, encounters and recordings in the port.

Out of these days came two presentations: Caminata Radial, a radio walk broadcast live from Playa Ancha on Radio Tsonami on the 9th of April, and a concert with Rodrigo Ríos Zunino, where the recordings of that week met his taut wires and feedback.
