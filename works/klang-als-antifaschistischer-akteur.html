Lefteris Krysalis/ Selected Works/ teaching

Klang als antifaschistischer Akteur im (Kunst-)Museum

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, WiSe 2025/26

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

With Salomé Voegelin

WiSe 2025/26

A course asking how sound can act against fascist and exclusionary structures in the art museum. Through readings, discussion, listening, site visits and sound interventions, students examined museum architecture, collections and curatorial practice.
