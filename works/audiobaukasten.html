Audiobaukasten – Introduction to Radio and Audio Production

Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, 2019–2025

Experimental Radio, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

Audiobaukasten I and II, Technische Grundlagen in der Audioproduktion

Co-taught with Maximilian Kraus, Jason Langheim and Y. Wang in some semesters

2019–2025

The introductory course in radio and audio production of the Experimental Radio chair, taught over several semesters between 2019 and 2025, first as a student tutor, from 2020 through teaching contracts and from 2022 as Artistic Associate. The course combined radio history and theory with acoustics and hands-on studio work: microphones, recording techniques and portable recorders, studio signal flow and the patchbay, editing in Reaper, interviewing, and the production of broadcasts in the studios of the Experimental Radio.

Audiobaukasten II continued with mixing and sound design, from levels, panning and effects to mixdown and mastering, and with the recording and transformation of acoustic material through synthesis, filtering, delay, reverb and modulation. Exercises in acousmatic listening accompanied the technical work, to hear how recording choices shape the perception of sound. In 2025 the technical part continued as Technische Grundlagen in der Audioproduktion, for more experienced students.
