Neuland 10 – Steinalt

Theater Erfurt, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, WiSe 2023/24

Experimental Radio, Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, and Kabosh Theatre Company, in co-production with Theater Erfurt STUDIO.BOX

Dramaturgy and production: Nathalie Singer, Frederike Moormann, Lefteris Krysalis, Paula McFetridge

WiSe 2023/24

Steinalt is a performative audio walk about ageing and how society might change to support it. It draws on life stories from senior residences near Theater Erfurt and introduces a fictional pharmaceutical company, TITHONEOS, which profits from anti-ageing products. The piece was part of the Neuland series at Theater Erfurt’s STUDIO.BOX and was funded through the New European Bauhaus project.
