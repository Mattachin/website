/teaching

From 2021 to 2026 I taught at Bauhaus University Weimar, initially through teaching contracts and, from 2022, as an Artistic Associate. My modules focused on radio transmission, soundscapes, field recording, and collaborative audio practices, often situated in public space and shaped by critical, experimental, and postdisciplinary methodologies.
